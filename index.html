Kedaipal: Website design / UIUX

Mobile-first storefront web application

Application

Selling from the chat

Kedaipal gives small sellers a storefront that lives where their buyers already are. Buyers check out from a link shared on Instagram, Threads or WhatsApp, and every order, payment and delivery lands on one screen the seller can run from their phone.

Creative contribution

Brand to product, end to end

I led Kedaipal's overall branding, shaping the design language and tone across both the landing page and the seller's web app. It set the experience end to end, from a visitor's first look at the landing page to the seller's screens.

Approach

Designed for the phone first

Many Malaysian SMEs sell their products and close their sales from their phones. So Kedaipal was designed mobile first, for ease of use: creating and editing a storefront happens right in the seller's pocket.

Aislelys: Web application design / UIUX

Wedding planner web application for two

Application

Planning the big day together

Aislelys keeps a couple's wedding planning in one shared place: an overview with the countdown, a planner where every task has an owner, the guest list, the budget, vendors and inspiration, all seen by both partners.

Creative contribution

Creator & Designer

I conceptualised Aislelys for friends who were getting married, and shaped it end to end, from branding to user experience. It blends familiar planning methods into one planner for brides and grooms.

Approach

Designed for two

Planning a wedding is shared work, so I approached it with my experience of designing project management applications, then gave the concept a softer, warmer touch.

SG Cards: Website design / UIUX

Trading card store locator for Singapore

Application

Every card shop in one place

SG Cards helps Singapore's trading card community find the shops they love. Players browse stores by region, see their opening hours and get directions, and keep up with TCG news, articles and events.

Creative contribution

Brand to product, end to end

A passion project I created for Singapore's growing TCG community. To make it easy to use, I made sure the experience is fully optimised for both web and mobile.

Approach

Cards first

Collectors think in cards, so the site does too. Each shop is a card that can be tapped open for its details and turned over for everything else: the games it carries, what it buys and sells, and its amenities.

Deqly: Website design / UIUX

A deck of cards in place of a single contact card

Application

More than one card

Deqly lets people share a deck of cards instead of a single contact card: their story, their work and their personality, all in one link that is easy to share at a networking event.

Creative contribution

Brand to product, end to end

I designed Deqly end to end, from its branding to the application experience. It meant rethinking how people exchange business cards: a new design, a fresh point of view and a new way to interact with the cards themselves.

Approach

Made to be interacted with

Having designed thousands of name cards, I wanted to create a new experience: one where companies and individuals can show more sides of themselves in a deck of cards, not just the two sides of a standard card.
